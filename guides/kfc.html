High-Protein, Low-Calorie KFC Combos Under 1,000 Calories

14 combos · Updated Aug 2026

KFC's grilled chicken is gone from the national menu, so the Original Recipe pieces and tenders do the heavy lifting here. Every combo below is a real order — an entrée, a side, and a drink, with a cookie where it earns its place — staying under 1,000 calories and hitting at least 30g of protein. Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Entrée (Priority 1) — the protein anchor. A single Original Recipe breast alone carries 39g; tenders and a second piece stack protein fast.
  2. Side (Priority 2) — green beans or corn to leave room for a second protein source; mashed potatoes, mac & cheese, or coleslaw when the budget allows.
  3. Drink (Priority 3) — zero-calorie by default (Diet Pepsi, unsweet tea). KFC has no milk option, so unlike McDonald's the drink slot can't add protein here — it all has to come from the food.
  4. Sweet (Priority 4) — a cookie, only when the combo has calorie room left. Several combos below skip it and put that budget into a second protein piece instead, which is the more realistic trade-off at KFC.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein KFC combo under 1,000 calories?
The Two-Piece Protein — an Original Recipe breast, an Original Recipe thigh, green beans, and a Diet Pepsi — comes to 695 calories with 59g of protein, the densest combo on this list.
Can you get a full KFC meal under 1,000 calories?
Yes, easily — every combo here lands between 415 and 830 calories, all well under the line, with most delivering 40g+ of protein.
What's the lowest-calorie full KFC order?
An Original Recipe breast with green beans and unsweet tea — 415 calories with 39g of protein, no dessert, for anyone keeping the rest of the day light.
What should I drink at KFC on a calorie budget?
Diet Pepsi, unsweet tea, or water — KFC doesn't offer a milk option like McDonald's, so the drink slot can't add protein here. All the protein in these combos comes from the chicken itself.
Is KFC's grilled chicken still on the menu?
Kentucky Grilled Chicken was pulled from most U.S. locations years ago and isn't part of KFC's current national lineup — check kfc.com/menu for your location, since this can vary.
