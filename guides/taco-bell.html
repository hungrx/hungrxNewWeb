High-Protein, Low-Calorie Taco Bell Combos Under 1,000 Calories

9 combos · Updated Aug 2026

The Cantina Chicken Bowl is the anchor here — one bowl carries 27g of protein on its own, and stacking multiple tacos gets you there just as reliably. Every combo below is a real Taco Bell order — an entrée or a few tacos, a side or extra, and a drink — staying well under 1,000 calories and clearing at least 30g of protein.

Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Entrée (Priority 1) — the Cantina Chicken Bowl is the single biggest protein anchor at 27g; three tacos of any style stack to the same range.
  2. Side/extra (Priority 2) — black beans add real protein (6g) for very few calories; the Cinnabon Delights or Cinnamon Twists appear only where the budget genuinely supports a treat.
  3. Drink (Priority 3) — zero-calorie by default (Diet Pepsi, water). Taco Bell doesn't have a milk option, so the drink slot doesn't add protein here.
  4. Sweet (Priority 4) — optional, not forced. Several combos below put that calorie room into black beans or a second taco instead, since that's the more realistic trade for someone tracking protein.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Taco Bell combo under 1,000 calories?
The Cantina Bowl & Taco — a Cantina Chicken Bowl, a Chicken Soft Taco, a Diet Pepsi, and Cinnamon Twists — comes to 820 calories with 39g of protein, and it still includes a real dessert.
Can you get a full Taco Bell meal under 1,000 calories?
Yes, with room to spare — every combo here lands between 420 and 820 calories.
Do two chicken tacos alone hit 30g protein at Taco Bell?
Not quite — two tacos plus a beans side or a third taco is usually what it takes. A pair of Chicken Soft Tacos with rice, for example, comes up short at 27g; swapping in beans or adding a third taco is what closes the gap.
What should I drink at Taco Bell on a calorie budget?
Diet Pepsi or water. Taco Bell doesn't have a milk option, so the drink slot can't add protein here — it all comes from the food.
What's the lowest-calorie way to hit 30g+ protein at Taco Bell?
The Triple Fresco Taco — three Fresco Chicken Soft Tacos and a Diet Pepsi — 420 calories with 33g of protein, no sides needed.
