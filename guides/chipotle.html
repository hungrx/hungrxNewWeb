High-Protein, Low-Calorie Chipotle Bowls Under 1,000 Calories

16 bowls · Updated Aug 2026

Chipotle doesn't have a "combo" in the McDonald's sense — no side, no drink menu, no dessert case. What it has is a build-your-own bowl, and that's actually the best format on this whole list for hitting big protein numbers at low calories. Every order below is a real Chipotle build — a protein pick, a base, and toppings — the way people actually order at the counter. All 16 stay well under 1,000 calories, and every single one clears 30g of protein, most by a wide margin.

Bowl Series

How We Built These Orders

Chipotle's menu doesn't map cleanly onto "main / side / drink / sweet" — there's no dessert case and drinks are just bottled or fountain, so we didn't force a fake sweet slot onto it. Instead, every order follows the way people actually build a bowl at the counter:

  1. Protein (the anchor) — a single scoop of chicken carries 32g on its own; go double-protein (two scoops of the same or different meat) and you can clear 60–70g before touching a base.
  2. Base — black or pinto beans add real protein (8g) on top of the protein choice; rice adds bulk and calories but little protein, so several orders below skip it entirely to keep the calorie count low.
  3. Veg & salsa — fajita veggies and salsa are close to free calorically and round out the bowl the way a real order looks.
  4. One extra — cheese (adds 6g protein), guacamole, or sour cream, used selectively rather than on every bowl, since guac in particular adds real calories for almost no protein.

Water or unsweetened iced tea is the default drink for all of these — Chipotle doesn't have a milk or protein-drink option like some of the other chains in this series, so the drink slot doesn't add anything nutritionally here.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Chipotle bowl under 1,000 calories?
Double chicken, black beans, fajita veggies, and fresh tomato salsa — 535 calories with 74g of protein. It's also the highest-protein order in this entire restaurant series, beating every other chain covered.
Can you get a full Chipotle order under 1,000 calories?
Yes, easily — every bowl on this list lands between 370 and 665 calories, leaving hundreds of calories of headroom even before you factor in chips or a drink.
Does Chipotle have a dessert?
No — Chipotle doesn't have a bakery case or dessert menu, so unlike the other chains in this series, there's no "sweet" slot to build into these orders. If you want something dessert-adjacent, guacamole is the closest thing to an indulgent add, though it's savory, not sweet.
What should I drink at Chipotle on a calorie budget?
Water or unsweetened iced tea. Chipotle doesn't have a milk or protein-drink option like some other chains, so unlike McDonald's or Chick-fil-A, the drink slot can't add protein here — it's purely a zero-calorie choice.
What's the lowest-calorie high-protein bowl at Chipotle?
Double barbacoa with fajita veggies, tomatillo salsa, and romaine lettuce — 380 calories with 49g of protein, no beans or rice needed.
