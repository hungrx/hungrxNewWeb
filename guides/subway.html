High-Protein, Low-Calorie Subway Combos Under 1,000 Calories

9 combos · Updated Aug 2026

Here's something most "healthy Subway" lists don't tell you: a single 6-inch sub, on its own, usually can't clear 30g of protein. Even the Italian B.M.T. and the Tuna fall short. Every combo on this list that actually hits the mark does it the way real customers with a protein goal order at Subway — by pairing a sub with a protein bowl (the no-bread version of the same fillings) instead of adding a bag of chips. All 10 combos below stay under 1,000 calories and clear at least 30g of protein.

Note: figures below are researched estimates pending verification against Subway's official nutrition data and the hungrX database.
Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Main protein — a 6-inch sub with your protein of choice. Rotisserie-style chicken is the strongest single option at 29g; most others sit in the high teens to low 20s.
  2. Second protein source — a protein bowl (the same fillings, no bread) is what actually gets these combos to 30g+. This is the single most useful move on this page — it's the difference between a combo that passes and one that doesn't.
  3. Side — apple slices or chips, kept light so the calorie budget goes to protein, not bread or extras.
  4. Drink — zero-calorie (diet soda, water). Subway doesn't have a milk option, so unlike McDonald's or Chick-fil-A the drink can't add protein here.

Subway's only real "sweet" is the cookie — it appears on the one combo where the calorie math supports it, and skips the others where a second protein source is the better trade.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Subway combo under 1,000 calories?
A Rotisserie-Style Chicken sub plus a Rotisserie Chicken protein bowl on the side, apple slices, and a diet soda — 555 calories with 58g of protein.
Can a single Subway sandwich alone hit 30g of protein?
Rarely. Most 6-inch subs land in the high teens to low 20s for protein — even the Italian B.M.T. and Tuna fall short. The reliable way to clear 30g at Subway is pairing a sub with a protein bowl (the same fillings without bread), not just picking a different sandwich.
What's a Subway protein bowl?
It's the same protein and toppings as a sandwich, served without bread — lower in calories, and a straightforward way to add a second protein source to an order without adding carbs.
What should I drink at Subway on a calorie budget?
Diet soda or water. Subway doesn't have a milk option like McDonald's or Chick-fil-A, so the drink slot can't contribute protein here.
What's the lowest-calorie way to hit 30g+ protein at Subway?
Two protein bowls — steak and turkey, no bread — at 315 calories with 42g of protein. It's not a traditional "sandwich order," but it's the leanest way to hit the number.
