Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  hungrX LLC, Delaware

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

How we use cookies

hungrX uses only essential cookies — cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to function. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

Essential cookies we use

Session: Keeps you logged in during your visit.
Preference: Remembers basic settings so the site works as expected.

Third-party cookies

We do not allow third-party cookies on our website. If you use the App Store or Google Play links, those platforms may set their own cookies subject to their own policies.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
