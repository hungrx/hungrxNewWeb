Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  hungrX LLC, Delaware

What we collect

hungrX collects the information you provide when you create an account (email address, name) and usage data generated by your interaction with the app (meals logged, restaurants viewed, app activity). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How we use it

We use your data to provide the hungrX service — showing you calorie and macro information for nearby restaurants, tracking your logged meals, and personalising your experience. We may use anonymised, aggregated data to improve the app and our restaurant guides.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely in the United States. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Third parties

We work with a small number of trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, payment processing) who are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@hungrx.com. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA.

Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies only. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of hungrX after changes constitutes acceptance.
