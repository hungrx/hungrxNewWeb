High-Protein, Low-Calorie Burger King Combos Under 1,000 Calories

7 combos · Updated Aug 2026

Straight talk: Burger King has the thinnest high-protein lineup in this series. There's no grilled chicken sandwich on the menu, and a single burger or chicken item plus fries usually falls short of 30g protein — fries add almost nothing on the protein side and eat a big chunk of the calorie budget. The 7 combos below are the ones that actually clear the bar, built the way you'd need to order to hit it: doubling up on protein (a double patty, or a burger plus a nugget side) rather than adding fries.

Note: figures below are researched estimates pending verification against Burger King's official nutrition data and the hungrX database.
Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Main (Priority 1) — a double-patty burger or the Royal Crispy Chicken carries the most protein per item; the Impossible Whopper is the plant-based option.
  2. Second protein (Priority 2) — a small burger or a nugget side is what closes the gap to 30g on several combos below, standing in for the traditional "side" slot.
  3. Drink (Priority 3) — zero-calorie by default. Burger King doesn't have a milk option, so the drink can't add protein here.
  4. Sweet (Priority 4) — a soft serve cone, only where the calorie math supports it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Burger King combo under 1,000 calories?
The Double Cheese & Jr — a Double Cheeseburger, a Whopper Jr, and a Diet Coke — comes to 710 calories with 38g of protein, the strongest combo Burger King's current menu supports.
Can you get a full Burger King meal under 1,000 calories?
Yes — every combo here lands between 520 and 885 calories. But hitting 30g of protein takes more effort at Burger King than at most other chains in this series; a single burger or sandwich with fries usually falls short.
Does Burger King have a grilled chicken sandwich?
Not currently on the national menu — Burger King's chicken lineup is the Royal Crispy Chicken (breaded) and Chicken Nuggets. Confirm at bk.com/menu, since this can change.
Why don't any of these combos include fries?
Fries add very little protein (about 3g) for a real chunk of calories. Every combo that swapped fries for a nugget side or a second small burger cleared 30g; the fries versions of the same combos consistently fell short.
What's the lowest-calorie way to hit 30g+ protein at Burger King?
The Double Hamburger & Nuggets — 520 calories with 31g of protein, no fries, no dessert.
