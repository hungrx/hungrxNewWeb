High-Protein, Low-Calorie McDonald's Combos Under 1,000 Calories

16 combos · Updated Aug 2026

Most "healthy McDonald's" lists give you one sandwich and call it a day. This guide gives you the full order — a main, a side, a drink, and a dessert — with every combo landing under 1,000 calories and packing at least 30g of protein. All 16 combos are built from the current McDonald's USA menu, ranked from highest protein to lowest, so you can walk up to the kiosk knowing exactly what to tap.

Combo Series

How We Built These Combos (Priority-Based Calorie Allocation)

Every combo follows the same calorie budget, allocated by priority:

  1. Main (Priority 1) — ~50% of calories. This is where the protein lives, so it gets the biggest share.
  2. Side (Priority 2) — ~15–25%. Fries when there's room, apple slices or a 4-piece McNuggets when we want to bank calories or add protein.
  3. Drink (Priority 3) — ~0–10%. Zero-calorie drinks (Diet Coke, Coke Zero, unsweetened iced tea) keep the budget open; 1% low-fat milk trades 100 calories for 8g of protein.
  4. Dessert (Priority 4) — ~15–20%. Vanilla cone (200 cal) or chocolate chip cookie (170 cal) — real desserts that don't blow the budget.

Two ordering tricks used throughout: swap fries for apple slices to free up ~215 calories for a bigger main, and add a 4-piece McNuggets as your side for 9g of extra protein at only 170 calories.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein McDonald's combo under 1,000 calories?
The Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese, apple slices, a Diet Coke, and a chocolate chip cookie — 925 calories and 50g of protein. Swapping fries for apple slices is what makes room for both the biggest burger on the menu and a dessert.
Can you get a full McDonald's meal — main, side, drink, and dessert — under 1,000 calories?
Yes. All 16 combos in this guide include all four items and stay under 1,000 calories, ranging from 685 to 990 calories. Every one delivers at least 30g of protein.
Can I fit a McFlurry into a 1,000-calorie McDonald's order?
Just barely. A regular Oreo McFlurry is around 510 calories, so it only works with a smaller main: a McDouble, apple slices, a Diet Coke, and an Oreo McFlurry comes to roughly 925 calories with 34g of protein.
What should I drink at McDonald's on a calorie budget?
Zero-calorie options — Diet Coke, Coke Zero, unsweetened iced tea, or water — keep your full budget for food. If you want more protein, 1% low-fat milk adds 8g of protein for 100 calories, the best protein-per-calorie drink on the menu.
What's the lowest-calorie full combo at McDonald's?
The Light Crispy: a McCrispy, apple slices, a Diet Coke, and a vanilla cone — 685 calories with 31g of protein, leaving over 300 calories of headroom under the 1,000-calorie line.
