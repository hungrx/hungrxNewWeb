High-Protein, Low-Calorie Wendy's Combos Under 1,000 Calories

11 combos · Updated Aug 2026

Wendy's has a genuine advantage over the other burger chains in this series: the Grilled Chicken Sandwich carries 34g of protein on its own, and the chili — a rare thing in fast food — adds real protein at very few calories. Every combo below is a real order, and this is one of the strongest pages in the whole series on protein numbers. All 11 stay under 1,000 calories and clear at least 30g of protein.

Note: figures below are researched estimates pending verification against Wendy's's official nutrition data and the hungrX database.
Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Main (Priority 1) — the Grilled Chicken Sandwich (34g) or Dave's Single (29g) anchor most combos here; chili can also serve as a main on its own.
  2. Side (Priority 2) — chili is the standout move at Wendy's: a small chili adds 15g of protein for just 240 calories, better than almost any side at any other chain in this series. Nuggets or a baked potato fill in where chili isn't the pairing.
  3. Drink (Priority 3) — zero-calorie by default. Wendy's doesn't have a milk option, so the drink slot can't add protein here.
  4. Sweet (Priority 4) — the Frosty, in Junior (200 cal) or Small (340 cal) size — Wendy's most iconic "treat," and one of the more protein-positive desserts in this series at 5–8g.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Wendy's combo under 1,000 calories?
The Grilled Chicken & Chili — a Grilled Chicken Sandwich, a small chili, a Diet Coke, and a Junior Frosty — comes to 790 calories with 54g of protein. It's also one of the only combos in this whole series that includes a real dessert and still clears 50g.
Can you get a full Wendy's meal under 1,000 calories?
Yes, easily — every combo here lands between 385 and 820 calories, most with 40g+ of protein and a Frosty included.
Is Wendy's chili actually high in protein?
Yes — a small chili has about 15g of protein for 240 calories, one of the best protein-per-calorie sides in fast food. It's a genuine point of difference from every other chain in this series.
Can I fit a Frosty into a high-protein Wendy's order?
Yes, more easily than at most chains — a Junior Frosty is 200 calories with 5g of protein, and most combos on this list have room for one without breaking 1,000 calories.
What's the lowest-calorie full combo at Wendy's?
The Grilled Chicken Lean: a Grilled Chicken Sandwich, apple bites, and a Diet Coke, no dessert — 385 calories with 34g of protein.
