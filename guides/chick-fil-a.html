High-Protein, Low-Calorie Chick-fil-A Combos Under 1,000 Calories

16 combos · Updated Aug 2026

Chick-fil-A's Grilled Nuggets are one of the best protein-per-calorie foods in fast food — a 12-count is roughly 38g of protein for under 200 calories. That makes this the strongest lineup in this whole series: every combo below is a real order — an entrée, a side, a drink, and a treat — staying under 1,000 calories and hitting at least 30g of protein, most well past it. Ranked highest protein first.

Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Entrée (Priority 1) — Grilled Nuggets or a Grilled Cool Wrap do the heavy lifting; the 12-count nuggets alone carry more protein than most fast-food entrées carry with a side attached.
  2. Side (Priority 2) — fruit cup or kale crunch to keep calories low; mac & cheese or a Greek yogurt parfait when there's budget for a second protein hit.
  3. Drink (Priority 3) — zero-calorie by default (diet lemonade, unsweetened tea, water); 1% milk trades 130 calories for 8g of protein, the same move that works well at McDonald's.
  4. Sweet (Priority 4) — an Icedream cone (170 cal) fits almost every combo; the chocolate chunk cookie (370 cal) is the bigger splurge and only appears where the budget allows it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Chick-fil-A combo under 1,000 calories?
The Grilled Dozen Max — 12-count Grilled Nuggets, a side salad, 1% milk, and a chocolate chunk cookie — comes to 850 calories with 58g of protein, the strongest combo in this whole series of restaurant pages.
Can you get a full Chick-fil-A meal under 1,000 calories?
Yes, with plenty of room to spare — every combo here lands between 250 and 850 calories, and most clear 40g+ of protein.
Are Chick-fil-A's Grilled Nuggets really that much better than the regular nuggets?
Yes. A 12-count order of Grilled Nuggets is roughly 190 calories with 38g of protein — nearly double the protein of the same-size regular fried nuggets for about the same calories.
What should I drink at Chick-fil-A on a calorie budget?
Diet lemonade, unsweetened iced tea, or water keep the full budget for food. 1% low-fat milk adds 8g of protein for 130 calories if you want the drink slot to contribute.
What's the lowest-calorie full combo at Chick-fil-A?
The Grilled Dozen (Leanest): 12-count Grilled Nuggets, a fruit cup, and unsweetened iced tea, no sweet — 250 calories with 39g of protein.
