High-Protein, Low-Calorie Panera Combos Under 1,000 Calories

9 combos · Updated Aug 2026

Panera customers don't really order a "main, side, drink, sweet" the way McDonald's customers do — the real order is You Pick Two: a half salad or half sandwich paired with a cup or bowl of soup. That's exactly how we built this list. Every combo below is a real You Pick Two pairing, staying under 1,000 calories and clearing at least 30g of protein. One honest flag first: Panera's bakery desserts are genuinely calorie-dense — a single chocolate chip cookie is over 400 calories — so most of these combos skip dessert entirely rather than force one in.

Combo Series

How We Built These Combos

  1. Half salad or half sandwich (the anchor) — the Green Goddess Caesar with Chicken is the strongest single option, carrying 35g in the full size or 17g in a half.
  2. Soup or chili (the pairing) — Turkey Chili is the standout here, adding 16–24g of protein depending on cup or bowl size, similar to how Wendy's chili performs in that chain's lineup.
  3. Side — an apple or a baguette, kept light.
  4. Drink — unsweetened tea, black coffee, or water. Panera doesn't have a milk-forward protein drink the way McDonald's or Chick-fil-A do.

On dessert: Panera's bakery case runs 400–500+ calories per item, which is high enough that adding one to most of these combos would break the 1,000-calorie line entirely. Rather than force it, most combos here leave that calorie room for a bigger main pairing instead — which matches how someone actually watching their numbers would order.

What Doesn't Work: The Cookie Problem

A single Panera chocolate chip cookie is roughly 410 calories — more than most entire sides in this series. Pairing a full-size high-protein salad with a cookie and a drink comes to over 1,050 calories, which is why none of the combos above include one. If you want a Panera bakery treat and still want to stay under 1,000 calories overall, the honest move is choosing a lighter main (a half salad or half sandwich instead of full-size) to make room — not adding it on top of a full-size main.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest-protein Panera combo under 1,000 calories?
The Full Caesar & Chili Cup — a full-size Green Goddess Caesar Salad with Chicken, a cup of turkey chili, and unsweetened tea — comes to 700 calories with 51g of protein.
What's the real way people order at Panera — is it main/side/drink/dessert like McDonald's?
Not really — most Panera orders are "You Pick Two": a half salad or half sandwich paired with a cup or bowl of soup. That's the structure every combo on this page follows, because it's how people actually order.
Can I get a Panera bakery treat and stay under 1,000 calories?
It's tight. A single cookie is around 410 calories, so pairing one with a full-size high-protein salad pushes the total over 1,000. If you want a treat, order a half salad or half sandwich instead of full-size to make the calorie room.
Is Panera's soup a good source of protein?
The turkey chili is the standout — a bowl carries 24g of protein, similar to how chili performs at Wendy's. Most other soups are lighter on protein.
What's the lowest-calorie way to hit 30g+ protein at Panera?
The Half Caesar & Half Fuji — two half-size salads, no soup — 610 calories with 32g of protein.
